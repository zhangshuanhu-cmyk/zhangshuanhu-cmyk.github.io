I am an economics researcher specializing in international trade, firm performance, and applied microeconometrics. My work uses quasi-experimental methods — staggered difference-in-differences, regression discontinuity, and instrumental variables — to estimate the causal effects of policy on firm-level outcomes.

My current project examines how China's WTO accession tariff liberalization affected firm total factor productivity, exploiting the staggered, sector-specific tariff reduction schedule as a source of quasi-random variation (Callaway & Sant'Anna 2021).

I target publication in top general-interest and field journals (AER, QJE, JPE, JIE, REStat).
